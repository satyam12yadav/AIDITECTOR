Executive Technical Dossier

AIDetector (Fake News Killer)

Automated Evidence-Grounded Fact Checking & Local Neural Credibility Architecture · v1.0 Production Architecture

1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

Traditional fake news detectors either rely on black-box generative AI models (which hallucinate, cost money per API call, and offer no proof) or crude keyword matching.

AIDetector's Core Innovation: Combines a fine-tuned local BERT transformer model (5ms latency, 0 cloud cost) with a live multi-source Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline and a Natural Language Inference (NLI) proposition engine. Every score is mathematically grounded in verifiable source citations.

3. Artificial Intelligence & NLP Models Used

⚡ Local BERT Classifier
Model: Pulk17/Fake-News-Detection
Engine: @xenova/transformers (ONNX)
Latency: ~5ms on standard CPU.
Benefit: 100% offline, zero API dependency.
🎯 Semantic NLI Proposition Engine
Function: Evaluates proposition entailment and mutual exclusivity without requiring literal words like "false" or "not".
🌐 Multi-Source RAG Retrieval
Providers: Exa.ai, Google FactCheck, 50+ Indian & Global wire services and fact-checking registries.

4. Credibility Scoring & Conflict Matrix

Verification Condition Stance Score Range System Decision Rule
Direct Unanimous Contradiction CONTRADICTS 5 – 15 / 100 Authoritative sources refute the claim proposition.
Direct Unanimous Support SUPPORTS 85 – 98 / 100 Independent Tier 1/2/3 sources corroborate the assertion.
Major False Claim + Minor True Facts CONTRADICTS 20 – 30 / 100 Contradiction on core proposition dominates the score.
No Reliable Evidence Found UNCLEAR 50 – 52 / 100 Neutral unverified baseline (absence of evidence is not falsehood).
Contested Convention (e.g. 6 vs 7 Continents) QUALIFIED ~55 / 100 Flags multi-model regional definitions with neutral nuance.
